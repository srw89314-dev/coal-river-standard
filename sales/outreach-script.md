# First outreach message

## Personal message

Hi [First name] — I’m launching Coal River Standard to help local businesses improve how they look and convert customers online.

I noticed [one specific, respectful observation about their current website or online presence]. I’d like to put together a free homepage direction showing how the business could look clearer and more professional on a phone.

There’s no obligation. If you like the direction, my founding-client website package is $750. If not, you still get a useful outside perspective.

Would you be open to seeing a homepage idea for [Business name]?

## Follow-up after four or five business days

Hi [First name] — just following up on the homepage idea for [Business name]. I’m keeping the first projects intentionally small and local, so I can give each one close attention. If improving the website is not a priority right now, no problem at all.

## Outreach rule

Personalize every message. Contact a small number of genuinely suitable businesses. Do not send mass messages or make claims about traffic, rankings, or revenue that cannot be supported.
