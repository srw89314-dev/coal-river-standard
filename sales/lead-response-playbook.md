# Lead-response playbook

## Response standard

- Reply during the same business day when practical.
- Personalize every response using the business name and the goal they submitted.
- Do not promise rankings, traffic, leads, revenue, or a launch date before reviewing the project.
- Move qualified leads toward one short conversation and a written proposal.

## First response

Subject: Coal River Standard — [Business name]

Hi [First name],

Thanks for telling me about [Business name]. I saw that you want the website to help with [briefly restate their goal]. I’m going to review the current online presence before suggesting a direction.

The next useful step is a few details about the business, the customers you most want to reach, and what visitors should be able to do. We can handle this by email, or arrange a short call if you prefer.

There is no obligation. If the project looks like a fit, I’ll send a clear scope, price, and timeline in writing before asking you to commit.

Sean  
Coal River Standard

## If the lead is not a fit

Hi [First name],

Thank you for reaching out. Based on what you need, I don’t think the current Coal River Standard services is the best fit. I’d rather be straightforward than push you into the wrong project.

[Optional: one useful next step or referral, only if genuine.]

I appreciate you considering Coal River Standard.

## Follow-up after three business days

Hi [First name],

I wanted to follow up on the website conversation for [Business name]. If improving the site is still a priority, I’d be glad to continue by email or arrange a short call. If the timing is not right, no problem at all.

## Qualification notes

Before proposing work, confirm:

- The business and primary contact are real and reachable
- The requested site fits a package from [the approved menu](offer.md), or needs custom discovery
- The owner can provide lawful content and photos
- One person can approve the work
- The price and payment schedule are realistic for the prospect
- Any ecommerce, booking, legal, accessibility, or regulated-industry needs are identified early
