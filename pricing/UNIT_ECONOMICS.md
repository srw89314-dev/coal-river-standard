# Optional analytics and care — unit economics worksheet (NOT approved prices)

Approved one-time website-build prices remain unchanged. Basic analytics setup may be included in a build only after provider, scope, privacy and test validation. Monthly analysis/reporting and ongoing care are separate optional services; do not advertise prices yet.

Monthly cost per client = provider fees allocated per site + hosting/monitoring/backup costs paid by CRS + (monthly service hours × internal hourly cost) + payment processing fees + contingency.

Required price before sales tax = monthly cost / (1 - target gross margin). Example only: $12 tools + 1.25 hours × $30 internal cost + $5 contingency = $54.50 cost. At a 50% target gross margin, illustrative price = $109/month. This is NOT a recommended or approved customer price.

Offer boundaries to decide:
- Basic setup: property configuration, owner access, test event, documentation; distinguish installation from ongoing reporting.
- Reporting add-on: one reviewed monthly report, one site, one period, agreed metrics, no ranking or lead guarantees.
- Care add-on: updates, backups, restore test cadence, response target and edit allowance; explicitly exclude 24/7 monitoring and unlimited changes unless separately costed.
- Client owns domain and data where practical; hosting and recurring vendor fees must be specified in scope.

Before price approval measure: provider costs, time for onboarding/reporting, support interruptions, number of sites, refund/cancellation burden, and actual tax/payment fees. Record at least three fictional workflow timings and one controlled test-site run, then seek owner approval.
