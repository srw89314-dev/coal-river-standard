## Included in standard website packages

Responsive layouts, foundational SEO, two consolidated rounds of revisions within scope, testing, a private review and basic handoff. The client provides accurate initial copy and permitted images; extensive writing, branding and photography are extra. Custom Systems follows its own written scope.

## Measurement requirement

Basic analytics setup, owner access and relevant contact-event tracking are intended to be included. Before accepting a project, confirm the provider, supported events, privacy requirements, costs and acceptance tests in its scope. No analytics provider or client reporting system is represented as already installed.

## Separate costs and services

Identify domain, hosting, software subscriptions, processing costs, approved external expenses and applicable taxes separately. Do not add a client card surcharge by default. Monthly reporting or care needs its own agreed scope and price; no recurring care price is set in this packet.

## Payment schedule

For standard packages, the proposed schedule is 50% to begin and 50% before launch, confirmed in the agreement. Custom work may use milestones. For a $995 service fee alone, those service installments are $497.50 each; taxes and approved expenses must be allocated separately. A starting payment is part of the total, not an extra charge.
