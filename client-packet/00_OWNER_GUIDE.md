Use this packet to prepare a website project from inquiry through handoff. Keep a blank master, make a private project copy, and send only the relevant client pages. Replace every project placeholder before use. The service agreement is prepared for legal review and is not ready for signature.

## Use the documents in this order

| Step | Document | Who completes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Questionnaire and feature discovery | Client supplies information; Sean qualifies scope |
| 2 | Proposal and statement of work | Sean prepares; client confirms direction and exact scope |
| 3 | Reviewed service agreement | Both parties accept the final versions before paid work |
| 4 | Invoice and payment record | Sean issues through the verified payment provider |
| 5 | Revision record and change order | Client supplies feedback; both approve extra scope |
| 6 | Launch approval | Client approves exact release; Sean completes release checks |
| 7 | Handoff and optional portfolio permission | Sean delivers; client acknowledges and chooses publicity |

## One project reference

Use one reference, such as CRS-2026-001, on every project document and invoice. Add a version to each proposal, scope, change order and approval. Keep accepted versions and dated approvals rather than overwriting them.

## Before sending a paid project agreement

Verify the legal seller and required business setup. Resolve the owner and legal review checklist at the end. Tax treatment, payment activation, support terms and private client storage remain separate launch tasks. This packet does not activate any account or authorize public launch.
