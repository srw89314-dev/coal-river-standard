## Scope and changes

We perform the deliverables specifically listed in the accepted statement of work. New pages, features, integrations or a changed direction require a written change order accepted by both parties before the additional work begins. The change order states the fee, revised dates and affected acceptance checks.

## Client materials and participation

The client supplies accurate business facts and materials it is authorized to use, appoints one approver, provides timely feedback and grants agreed delegated access. We record missing inputs and agree on revised dates where those inputs affect the schedule. We do not request passwords by ordinary email.

## Fees and third party costs

The SOW lists the exact service fee, payment milestones, confirmed applicable taxes and approved external expenses. The proposed standard schedule is 50% to begin and 50% before launch. We obtain written approval before adding a paid asset or subscription. Provider-hosted invoices are used for payments; no card details belong in this packet.

## Review and acceptance

Standard packages include two consolidated rounds of revisions within the SOW. The SOW defines review dates and observable acceptance checks. The client approves the exact release in writing before public launch or agreed DNS changes. Silence does not create acceptance.
