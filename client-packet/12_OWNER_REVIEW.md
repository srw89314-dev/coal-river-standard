Resolve before the first paid project

Task 2 delivers consolidated blank templates. It does not complete business approvals, legal review, payment setup, client storage or a live project rehearsal.

## Owner decisions

- [ ] Exact seller identity formation details notice address and required approvals verified.
- [ ] Support channel client review period correction window response target and exclusions chosen.
- [ ] Domain hosting accounts fees renewal payer and backup responsibilities chosen.
- [ ] Analytics provider supported events privacy owner access and costs validated before promising delivery.
- [ ] Tax treatment payment provider and invoice calculations checked before live billing.
- [ ] Private project storage and document version process ready before actual client records.
## Agreement review

- [ ] Scope precedence ownership transfer and reusable material license completed.
- [ ] Starting payment cancellation refunds suspension termination and notice rules reviewed.
- [ ] Confidentiality privacy incidents accessibility roles warranty and remedies reviewed.
- [ ] Liability allocation disputes governing law and electronic acceptance reviewed.
- [ ] No unresolved LEGAL REVIEW placeholders remain in a version offered for signature.
## Acceptance and retention

Record the exact accepted versions, authorized approvers and dates. Preserve signed documents and approval evidence privately. Do not treat a proposal, payment screenshot or unanswered message as acceptance of all terms.

## Primary references for the reviewer

West Virginia Code sections 39A-1-5 through 39A-1-9 address electronic transactions and attribution. Electronic form alone does not invalidate a record; the full transaction requirements and circumstances still matter. https://code.wvlegislature.gov/39A-1/

17 USC section 204 addresses signed written transfers of copyright ownership. Have the reviewer distinguish file delivery from the exact rights conveyed. https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html

References checked November 15 2026. They do not establish that these draft terms are enforceable or complete for a specific client.
