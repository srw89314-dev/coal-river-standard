# Coal River Standard

Revenue-focused launch site and sales materials for a local-business web design service.

## Before public launch

1. Add a Netlify Forms email notification after the owner confirms the destination inbox.
2. Confirm the founding-client price and included scope.
3. Confirm the public service area and business contact details.
4. Add the purchased domain only after it is registered in the owner's name.
5. Replace the illustrative preview with the first approved client example when available.

The lead form is handled by Netlify Forms and stored in the site's Netlify account. The production site remains protected by Netlify team login until the owner explicitly approves a public launch.

The site intentionally contains no invented testimonials, customer counts, awards, or portfolio claims.

## Sales materials

- `sales/offer.md` — package, price, boundaries, and founding-client positioning
- `sales/outreach-script.md` — personalized first message and follow-up
- `sales/client-intake.md` — information and approvals needed to begin
- `sales/proposal-template.md` — ready-to-customize client proposal
- `sales/lead-response-playbook.md` — reply sequence for new inquiries
- `sales/launch-day-plan.md` — final private-to-public release sequence
