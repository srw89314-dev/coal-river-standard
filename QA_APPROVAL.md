# Coal River Standard final approval

Complete these checks on the protected Netlify site before approving a public launch.

## Phone approval

Use a real phone, not only a narrowed desktop window.

- Open the homepage while signed in to the authorized Netlify account
- Confirm the logo, headline, preview image, buttons, pricing, FAQs, form, and footer fit without sideways scrolling
- Tap every navigation and call-to-action link
- Open and close every FAQ
- Confirm text is comfortably readable without zooming
- Confirm form fields are easy to tap and the keyboard does not cover the active field
- Rotate the phone once and confirm the page returns to a clean layout
- Read the offer, price, payment schedule, and care-plan details as a prospective client
- Record any wording or visual change needed before launch

## Tested inquiry notification

This test confirms that a real prospect will not disappear silently.

1. Add the new business email as the Netlify Forms notification recipient.
2. Submit the protected website form using a real secondary email address, a believable business name, and a full-sentence project goal. Avoid obvious test text because spam filtering may flag it.
3. Confirm the submission appears under the `website-inquiry` form in Netlify.
4. Confirm the notification arrives at the business inbox.
5. Confirm the message includes name, business, city/state, email, current site, and project goal.
6. Confirm replying targets the submitter's email address.
7. If it is missing, check the Netlify spam list and the business inbox spam folder before changing the form.
8. Delete or clearly label the test record after the workflow is verified.
