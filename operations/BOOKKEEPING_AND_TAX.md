# Bookkeeping and tax preparation
Prepared November 15, 2026. Workbook formulas are operational cash records. Tax classification and filing setup remain unresolved.

## Working reserve rule
Use an editable 30% of service receipts less service refunds, excluding separately collected sales tax, as an initial cash reserve target. This is a savings rule rather than a prediction of the tax owed. The workbook's Settings cell can be changed if Sean or his adviser chooses another percentage. It uses receipts before costs deliberately; a profit-based estimate requires the broader tax calculation.

Sales tax collected is tracked separately. Moving cash to a money-market account does not remit tax. Keep federal/state income and self-employment reserves distinct in the records from sales tax and city B&O obligations, even if one bank holds the cash.

IRS estimated-tax rules consider total household tax, withholding, credits and prior-year tax. Additional wage withholding can sometimes cover side-business tax. Do not choose installment amounts solely from a percentage of website sales. Confirm the first applicable payment period, required amounts and any annualized-income approach with the adviser. No filing calendar or automatic payments were created.

## Use the workbook
- Clients, Contacts and Sites hold the rolodex. Projects holds package, stage, approver and scope reference. Actions and Documents track follow-ups, versions and approval evidence.
- Invoices is an internal register. It does not issue an invoice. Leave status Draft until business, tax and payment setup are ready. The invoice total adds service, tax and approved billed expenses.
- Money records the checking cash side. Enter positive amounts and choose the transaction type. Service Amount excludes separately collected sales tax; record the processor fee once in its column. When a provider batches transactions, reconcile allocations to the actual bank payout and retain the provider detail. Do not enter both the component receipts and the payout as additional sales. This workbook does not reconcile an unsettled processor balance automatically.
- Reserve records actual reserve-account cash. Transfers to/from checking must appear on both ledgers and match. Tax payments from Reserve stay there; do not duplicate the same payment in checking. Sales-tax remittances and income-tax payments need distinct evidence and notes.
- Operating costs include entered expenses less expense refunds plus separately entered payment fees. Cash operating surplus is not taxable income: owner draws, tax treatment, startup costs, assets and timing require separate review.
- Time is actual hours by project. It supports pricing/margin review without treating Sean's time as a cash tax deduction.
- Match every record to a bank, processor, receipt or approval reference. Use unique IDs. Duplicate IDs are highlighted. Prepared formulas cover 100 rows per table; extend ranges and validation together before exceeding this.

Digital delivery alone does not establish an exemption. WV's current TSD 300 describes a general presumption of taxability and specific exemptions. An older official 2003 appeal synopsis specifically distinguishes website/hosting/advertising work from exempt data processing. That old case is a reason to ask a specific classification question, not a final ruling for today's packages. No tax exemption or universal rate has been hardcoded into customer invoices.

## Weekly close
Reconcile checking, reserve and processor records; match fees/refunds; clear missing dates/types/evidence; review unpaid invoices and follow-ups; record reserve transfers; export a dated copy. Monthly, review actual hours and costs before changing prices. Capture due dates only from issued notices or verified requirements.
