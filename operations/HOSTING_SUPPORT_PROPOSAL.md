## If care is offered later

If a 30-minute monthly edit allowance is chosen, define eligible edits, whether time rolls over, approval of overage, minimum billing increments and excluded redesign/new features. Do not promise unlimited support.

Record billing frequency, renewal, cancellation notice, prepaid periods, refunds, offboarding timeline, access transfer and backup retention in the reviewed agreement. Do not set automatic renewals or cancellation penalties from this internal proposal.
