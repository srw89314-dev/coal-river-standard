The specific checking and money-market product disclosures list $10 monthly for paper statements. The general product overview lists $5 for money-market paper statements. Select electronic statements and ask the banker to reconcile the inconsistency against the account-opening disclosure.

The reserve account should be the Business Money Market, not Super Savings by accident: the overview lists a $5 Super Savings service fee unless its $200 balance condition is met. Primary status does not lock the money-market APY. No fixed introductory expiration was stated on the reviewed money-market product page; it is still a variable rate.

## Prepare privately before applying
These are preparation items, not a verified bank-specific complete checklist: issued formation record and effective date, EIN confirmation, current registration/license documents, adopted operating agreement if requested, owner/signatory identity and address evidence, business activity and expected transaction volumes, ownership/control information, and the source of the $100 opening funds. Submit identifiers only through the bank's verified secure process.

The IRS instructs LLCs to form with the state before requesting an EIN. A submitted fee receipt does not establish that approval has issued. Climate First's public pages reviewed here did not settle whether it requires a pending city's license or the complete documents for a West Virginia single-member LLC. Those remain banker questions; do not claim that all licenses are either required or unnecessary.

## Exact questions for the banker
1. Can a West Virginia home-based, single-member, member-managed web and graphic design LLC open both accounts entirely remotely?
2. Please provide the exact required documents, acceptable EIN evidence, ownership/control and signatory verification requirements. Is an operating agreement or certificate of existence required?
3. May the city license be pending? Which issued state formation and tax-registration documents must be available first?
4. Are $50 per account and $0 monthly service fees with e-statements still the opening terms? Are there funding-source or new-account charges?
5. What establishes and maintains the primary checking relationship for a new business with irregular revenue? Is there a minimum activity test, review interval or grace period?
6. What APY will appear in today's disclosure? Is any portion promotional or time-limited? Please resolve the $5 versus $10 paper-statement discrepancy in published pages.
7. Can checking receive Stripe payouts? What ACH holds, transfer limits, mobile-deposit limits and fees apply, and may the money market transfer promptly to checking for taxes?
8. What process closes the accounts or transfers reserves if the business does not proceed?

Record banker name, date, answers and written disclosure reference. Opening and funding remain Sean's action after readiness is verified.
