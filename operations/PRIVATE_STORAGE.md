## Retention and independent backup
Review inactive inquiries after 90 days and collect only needed information. Do not automatically delete contracts, approvals, finance/tax or dispute records; confirm retention periods with the relevant adviser. Suspend deletion during a dispute or required preservation period.

After an important approval and at least weekly during active work, export a dated copy to an encrypted owner-controlled device. Keep an additional independent encrypted copy on a separate owner-controlled backup destination. Do not treat another folder in the same Drive as disaster recovery. Never overwrite the only accepted version. Maintain a manifest of filenames, versions, dates and hashes where available.

Before real client use, restore a harmless sample into an isolated folder, open its documents, reconcile an invoice and confirm access restrictions. Monthly, repeat a sample restore and record result, date and missing files.
