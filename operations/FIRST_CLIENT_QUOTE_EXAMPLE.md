# Cedar Ridge Repair — fictional quote walkthrough

Project CRS-TEST-001 | Proposal P1 | Scope S1 | Internal rehearsal

## Intake and recommendation

The owner wants to describe repair services and route visitors to a quote request. The Business package fits the five informational pages and single form. A proposed appointment system is outside this scope and would have its own quote and approval.

Before quoting a real client, confirm the service area, authorized approver, who owns the domain and hosting, who receives inquiries, and whether approved copy and photos are available. Ask whether a quote request or a booking is the intended next step.

## Deliverables and acceptance

| Deliverable | How to check it |
| --- | --- |
| Home, Services, About, FAQs and Contact | All five are reachable through the site navigation on phone and desktop. Approved business details match the client brief. |
| Quote form | Empty required fields block submission. A controlled inquiry reaches the agreed inbox, preserves the visitor’s Reply-To and shows a confirmation. |
| Responsive layout and basic search setup | No horizontal scrolling at the agreed test widths. Pages have distinct titles and one main heading each. |
| Two consolidated revision rounds | Each round has one dated feedback list and a matching preview version. Requests outside scope use a separate change order. |
| Handoff | Approver names the exact release. Client verifies owner access and receives the site files or agreed export, renewal details and support instructions. |

## Price and dependencies

Build fee $995. The proposed two installments on the service fee are $497.50 each. Taxes, hosting and domain charges are unresolved in this exercise, so there is no final amount due. The first installment would be due before work begins and the second before launch, subject to the signed terms. Use the [invoice template](../client-packet/07_INVOICE_AND_PAYMENT_RECORD.md) with the verified provider.

The rehearsal excludes booking, payments on the site, branding and copywriting. Before a real quote goes out, complete the [scope template](../client-packet/04_STATEMENT_OF_WORK.md) with dates, content dependencies, the approved analytics deliverable if applicable, the agreed support window and the approved agreement version. This internal sample carries no client acceptance or payment request.
