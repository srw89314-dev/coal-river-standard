# First-client rehearsal — fictional test, not a real sale

**Purpose:** Prove the website-build business can accept, scope, build, review, invoice and hand off a project without relying on memory or making unsupported promises. Use fictional business **Cedar Ridge Repair**; fictional contact **Taylor Example**. Never send real payment requests or emails during rehearsal.

| Gate | Test action | Pass evidence | Current state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Inquiry | Choose New informational website; complete guided contact form | Submission stored in Netlify; category and Reply-To verified | Previous real email test passed; full new rehearsal pending |
| 2. Qualification | Classify need, ownership, goals, pages, budget, content, deadline | Internal record with dated response and next action | Packet exists; CRM not deployed |
| 3. Scope | Quote approved Business package $995 with exact included deliverables | Proposal and scope have consistent price, revision count, exclusions | Templates prepared; owner/legal review pending |

## Mandatory sign-off
Do not mark the full rehearsal passed until every applicable gate has dated evidence. No fabricated client testimonials, bookings, payments or measured results. Keep test data clearly fictional and avoid storing passwords, EIN, card numbers or client records in source control.

## First-client minimum gate
- [ ] EIN and business registration/licensing obligations resolved
- [ ] Signed operating agreement and employer outside-business clearance
- [ ] Business bank and bookkeeping ready
- [ ] Approved service agreement, ownership/support terms, scope and invoice process
- [ ] Secure client-file storage, even if CRM is manual initially
- [ ] Functional inquiry route and verified response workflow
- [ ] End-to-end fictional project with test payment and handoff
- [ ] Owner-approved website/public access and outreach plan

**Important:** A sophisticated CRM, automatic monthly reports and a 62-industry design builder are useful improvements, but need not block a first basic website client if a secure manual workflow is demonstrably reliable.
